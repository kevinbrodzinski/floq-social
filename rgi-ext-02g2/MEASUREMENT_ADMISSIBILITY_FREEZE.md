## Fixed-work response observable

Every replicate is a fresh process. The process creates deterministic FP32 1024×1024 operands, records a no-pressure baseline, then allocates and touches the quantum-aligned pressure buffer, leaves it resident, waits two seconds, and repeats exactly the same matmul. Each phase uses two unrecorded warmups plus five CUDA-event timings.

For retained block `b` and level `p`:

`D_b(p) = L_pressure_b(p)/L_baseline_b(p) - 1`

`U(p) = median_b D_b(p)`.

The response is therefore an independently timed fixed-work degradation. Allocated bytes, requested p, fill time, or any quantity mechanically proportional to p is forbidden as the scientific response. If the resulting U is not strictly positive and coherently scale-identifiable, the alpha instrument refuses.
